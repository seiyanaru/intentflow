# 260627 V3: Lee2019 second-dataset longitudinal/source-side subspace check

## One-line verdict

Second dataset check succeeded operationally, but it changed the scientific story.

```text
守れる:
  source-side supervised tangent-subspace selection improves zero-target-label
  cross-session EEG-MI on a second dataset.

守れない:
  longitudinal same-class drift penalty is the universally best/essential ingredient.
```

On Lee2019, the best fixed method is `source_only_q0.25`, not `longitudinal_q0.10`.
This is good for "source-side subspace selection" but bad for a paper whose title is
too strongly "longitudinal".

## Protocol

Dataset:

```text
Lee2019_MI
left vs right motor imagery
54 subjects
2 sessions per subject
100 trials/session
62 channels after MOABB LeftRightImagery
```

Evaluation:

```text
held-out unit: subject
train decoder: held-out subject session0 labels
test: held-out subject session1 trial 21 onward
target labels: not used for held-out subject
target prefix: first 20 session1 trials, unlabeled EA reference only
feature: broad 8-30 Hz covariance tangent, 62 channels
classifier: LDA shrinkage=auto
```

Source-side subspace statistics:

```text
For held-out subject s:
  learn feature scores from all non-held subjects' session0 -> session1 labeled structure.
  select top q fraction of tangent dimensions.
  fit held-out subject session0 LDA only on selected dimensions.
  evaluate held-out subject session1.
```

## Interpretation

### What replicated from Stieger

Random top-k collapses:

```text
random_q0.25: -6.209 pp
random_q0.10: -9.470 pp
```

So the improvement is not because "fewer dimensions are always better".
The selected tangent subspace contains meaningful source-side MI information.

Source-side subspace restriction improves a second dataset:

```text
source_only_q0.25:
  +2.176 pp vs full
  CI excludes 0
```

This is a real positive result.

### What did not replicate

The Stieger-specific claim was:

```text
longitudinal stability term adds improvement beyond source-only discriminability.
```

Lee2019 does not support this.

```text
longitudinal_q0.10 - source_only_q0.25 = -0.324 pp
CI crosses 0 widely
```

Therefore, a title/story that puts `longitudinal` as the universal central mechanism is too strong.

## Revised research strategy

### Strongest honest paper axis now

```text
Source-side subspace selection for zero-target-label cross-session EEG-MI.
```

Longitudinal stability becomes an optional dataset-dependent scoring term:

```text
Stieger:
  longitudinal score > source-only score

Lee2019:
  source-only score >= longitudinal score
```

The robust cross-dataset phenomenon is not "longitudinal drift penalty".
It is:

```text
Use labels from source subjects/sessions to select a compact Riemann tangent subspace
before zero-label target-session evaluation.
```

### Novelty risk

This is weaker novelty than the original longitudinal claim.
Source-only q0.25 is basically supervised feature selection in tangent space.
The paper must therefore position itself carefully:

```text
Not: we invented feature selection.

Instead:
  in cross-session EEG-MI, a simple source-side subspace selection baseline
  beats more elaborate unsafe adaptation/frontier ideas and transfers across datasets.
```

That can be a strong empirical paper/workshop paper.
For journal-level novelty, we still need either:

```text
1. a sharper method than source-only Fisher-style selection, or
2. a mechanistic explanation of when longitudinal drift helps vs hurts, or
3. another multi-session dataset with more than two sessions where longitudinal term replicates.
```
